What the diagnostic tests

  • SKU velocity, stock cover and stockout risk
  • OTIF, delay causes and warehouse dwell time
  • Territory, agent and outlet productivity
  • Customer cohorts, reorder cycles and churn risk
  • Discount, return and margin leakage
  • Route utilisation and cost-to-serve
  • Collections, receivables and cash-conversion delays

Decision layer

  • Reallocate inventory before high-value SKUs stock out
  • Prioritise routes by margin and service-risk impact
  • Flag outlets whose reorder pattern is deteriorating
  • Identify agents with high volume but poor margin quality
  • Surface shipments likely to breach SLA before failure occurs
  • Automate executive summaries and exception alerts

Engagement structure

1. Diagnose

Map data sources, KPIs, leakage points and decision latency.

2. Prove

Build a focused analytical layer around one expensive operating problem.

3. Automate

Add alerts, workflows, agents and recurring reporting only where ROI is measurable.

4. Measure

Track savings, recovered revenue, cycle time, service levels and adoption.
