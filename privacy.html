When you submit the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to FormSubmit for email delivery; FormSubmit states that submissions may be retained for 30 days. The CV assistant answers from public portfolio content in your browser. By default, no System-One endpoint is configured. If the site owner configures the optional System-One endpoint, the question you type may be sent to that endpoint only for advisory topic and prompt-safety classification; it is not used as factual CV evidence. Do not submit passwords, secrets, private records, or other sensitive information through the assistant. The service worker stores public portfolio files on your device for offline use.
