1. Executive diagnosis

The portfolio is broadly performing, but aggregate repayment hides three monetizable control gaps: repeat-borrower decay after the first successful cycle, concentrated late exposure in a small borrower cohort, and collection effort that is not consistently prioritized by recoverable value. The audit converts those gaps into measurable interventions rather than a descriptive dashboard.

2. KPI framework

3. Example cohort findings

Cohort A — clean repeat: 5+ completed cycles, no unresolved late balance. Candidate for controlled limit expansion and lower-touch servicing.

Cohort B — cured late: repaid after delinquency but continues trading. Preserve access while slowing limit growth and measuring recurrence.

Cohort C — first-cycle friction: late/default behavior before a second successful cycle. Prioritize onboarding, affordability and early collection intervention instead of blanket acquisition spend.

5. Delivery package

A production engagement converts source-system data into a reconciled credit ledger, cohort and vintage analysis, borrower/seller segmentation, collections prioritization, retention diagnostics, limit-policy evidence, exception controls and an executive decision pack. Each metric is tied to a source definition and an operational action.

6. Success measures

Track cure rate, recovered KES per collection hour, roll-rate into delinquency, repeat-buyer rate, default/write-off rate, risk-adjusted GMV, limit-increase performance and reactivation lift. Baselines are frozen before intervention so changes can be attributed rather than assumed.

Data note: This public sample contains synthetic values only. A client audit uses authorized data, documented definitions and access controls; sensitive borrower information is not published in the deliverable.
