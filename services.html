FIT

When we should — and should not — work together.

Good fit

  • You have an expensive manual or analytical bottleneck.
  • A decision-maker can define what success is worth.
  • You can provide legitimate access to the required systems/data.
  • You want production deployment, not an AI demo.

Not a fit

  • There is no measurable business problem.
  • The project depends on unauthorized data or access.
  • You want automation without accountability or controls.
  • You cannot identify who owns the outcome.
